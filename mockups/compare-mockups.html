Today vs yesterday

Five ways to compare today with yesterday, with days running midnight to midnight. Each mockup compares today so far with yesterday at the same time (2:30 PM), and also shows yesterday's full-day total. All numbers are made-up example data, not Aveer's.

Example: it's 2:30 PM. Today so far: 6 feeds (4 breastfeeds, 1 h 42 min; 1 formula, 45 mL; 1 breast milk bottle, 40 mL), 4 wet and 2 dirty. Yesterday by 2:30 PM: 5 feeds, 3 wet, 1 dirty. Yesterday's full day: 9 feeds, 7 wet, 3 dirty. Arrows show direction only, in neutral colors, because more isn't automatically better. The rings show progress toward the daily targets.
