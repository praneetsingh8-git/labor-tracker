Aveer's daily scorecard

Five ways to show one day of feeds and diapers. Every mockup uses the same example day (Day 6, 4:24 AM to now), not Aveer's real data, so you can compare layouts directly. Pick a letter, or mix parts.

The example day: 10 feeds (6 breastfeeds totalling 2 h 50 min, 2 formula bottles of 95 mL, 2 breast milk bottles of 75 mL) and 8 diapers (5 wet only, 2 wet + dirty, 1 dirty only). Targets shown are the typical guides from day 5 on: 8+ feeds, 6+ wet, 3+ dirty. Hover or tap a bar segment or block to see its details.
