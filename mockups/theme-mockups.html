Aveer's app, three gentle themes

Three gender-neutral themes, each shown in day and night. The app would switch automatically with your iPhone's light/dark setting. Every screen is the layout you chose: Today / Log / Trends tabs with separate Feeding, Diapers and Weight cards for quick entry, plus the new Next feed card. Example data only.

The colors for breast, formula and breast milk, and for wet, wet + dirty and dirty, are the same in all three themes (slightly brighter at night) and pass colour-blind and contrast checks on every background. Only the backgrounds, text and accent change between themes.
