Aveer tab layouts

Five full-page layouts for the Aveer tab. Every one keeps the rings with the yesterday tick (option A) at the top and the charts at the bottom; they differ in how you log entries and where details live. Days run midnight to midnight. All numbers are example data, not Aveer's.

Every layout keeps editing the same way: tap any entry to change its time, side, amount or remark, and swipe or tap Delete to remove it. "Log a past feed" stays in every version for feeds you forgot to start.
